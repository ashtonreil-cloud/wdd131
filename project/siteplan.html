Webkinz Mines Home Page

This webpage is all about the Webkinz mines: the gems, their rarity, which mines they are in, the man who runs the mines (Arte Fact), as well as the Crown of Wonder.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Crown of Wonder?

The Crown of Wonder is a legendary, wearable clothing item in Webkinz that players unlock by completing the daily Gem Hunt minigame in the Curio Shop.

Who is Arte Fact?

Arte Fact is the owner of the Curio Shop and runs the mines.

How many mines are there?

There are exactly 5 gem mines in the Webkinz Curio Shop. Each mine corresponds to specific gem colors and contains one unique primary gem.

How many gems are needed for the Crown of Wonder?

You need 30 unique gems to complete the collection and earn the Crown of Wonder.
