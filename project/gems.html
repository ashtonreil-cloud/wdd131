The Complete Gem Index & Rarity Hierarchy Gems are structured like a pyramid. Every color family has 1 Rare (top of the pyramid, exclusive to 1 mine), 2 Uncommons (middle level, found in 2 separate mines), and 3 Commons (bottom level, found across 3 separate mines). ⚪ White Gem Family • Webkinz Diamond (Rare): The holy grail of Webkinz World. • Base Value: 400–600 KinzCash (Up to 1,000 as Gem of the Day). • Unicorn Horn (Uncommon): A spiral-shaped crystal. • Yum Zum Sparkle (Uncommon): Named after the magical woodland creature. • Booger Nugget (Common): Despite its unappealing name, it is a frequent find. • Goober Glitter (Common): Shimmering atomic-shaped dust particle. • Zingoz Zincoz (Common): A round stone shaped like a Zingoz.

🔴 Red Gem Family • Red Ruby Heart (Rare): A bold, heart-shaped crimson crystal. • Base Value: 45–540 KinzCash (Up to 900 as Gem of the Day). • Ember Amber (Uncommon): Resembles glowing sunset tree sap. • Volcano Viscose (Uncommon): Radiant red like moving lava. • Flare Fyca (Common): A bright flame-shaped stone. • Torch Treasure (Common): High-sparkle common stone. • Lava Lump (Common): Rough, jagged volcanic rock.


🟢 Green Gem Family • Earth Emerald (Rare): Brilliant deep-green geometric block. • Jaded Envy (Uncommon): Smooth, polished pale jade cylinder. • Stalagmite Spike (Uncommon): Jagged and sharp spike structure. • Cat’s Eye Crystal (Common): Features a distinct slit-pupil shimmer across its center. • Pearl Egg (Common): Smooth oval with an iridescent green hue. • Terra Tectonic (Common): A layered, rocky geode fragment.


🔵 Blue Gem Family • Ocean Sapphire (Rare): A pristine teardrop blue gemstone. • Sea Stone (Uncommon): Tumbled marine-blue pebble. • Teardrop Tower (Uncommon): Elongated blue stalactite shard. • Aqua Orb (Common): Perfectly spherical water-colored marble. • Rainbow Flower (Common): Shimmers blue with multi-colored floral undertones. • River Ripple (Common): Flat, smooth stone featuring concentric water rings.


🟡 Yellow Gem Family • Corona Topaz (Rare): Crowned starburst sun-stone. • Aurora Rox (Uncommon): Streaked with neon yellow northern-light bands. • Pyramid Plunder (Uncommon): A miniature golden triangular pyramid. • Carat Eclipse (Common): Yellow crescent shape masking a dark stone. • Lemon Drop (Common): Translucent sugary yellow crystal. • Starlight Shimmer (Common): Four-point yellow star cluster.

🤝 The Clubhouse Strategy: Doug the Dog Once you accumulate duplicate gems, never sell them back to Arte Fact unless they are the Gem of the Day. Instead, take them to the Clubhouse Collector’s Room to trade with Doug the Dog. How Doug's Quests Work • Doug assigns a gem quest asking for specific stones. Early levels start with a single common gem, but as you scale through his 20 total reward levels, he will demand combinations of multiple uncommons or rares at once. • The Progress Penalty: If you click through his dialogue and officially fail/deny two consecutive quests because you don't have the gems, Doug drops you down a progress level and hands you standard KinzCash instead of rare loot. The "Disappoint" Loophole Multi-Pet Reset Trick Doug's trades are locked to one per pet per day. However, if you have a massive stockpile of gems in your dock, you can cycle through different pets on your account, walk right back into the Clubhouse, and immediately complete the next tiers of quests in succession on the exact same day. Once you win his 20th final prize, your profile resets back to Level 1 so you can farm the rare item chain all over again.
